Who We Are

StraNova Holdings is a privately held healthcare services and business development company focused on building, supporting, and developing specialized healthcare organizations that improve the way care is delivered, managed, and supported.

StraNova selectively invests in and supports exceptional, ethical, and highly compliant healthcare companies that demonstrate a commitment to integrity, accountability, operational excellence, and the communities they serve.

Our focus is on healthcare organizations that contribute meaningful value to the healthcare ecosystem, helping providers operate more effectively, strengthening financial and operational performance, supporting better patient experiences, and enabling healthcare professionals to focus on delivering quality care.

Through its operating companies, StraNova provides strategic guidance, business expertise, operational support, and resources designed to strengthen healthcare organizations and create sustainable, long-term value.

What We Look For

  • Clinical Excellence

    Organizations committed to delivering exceptional patient care, improving outcomes, and maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and service.

  • Integrity & Compliance

    Leaders who embrace ethical business practices, regulatory compliance, accountability, and a culture built on trust, transparency, and responsibility.

  • Data-Driven Decision Making

    Organizations that leverage data, analytics, performance reporting, and measurable insights to improve patient outcomes, optimize operations, and strengthen financial performance.

  • Revenue Cycle Excellence

    Healthcare organizations dedicated to improving billing accuracy, maximizing reimbursement, reducing waste, and supporting the long-term financial stability of providers and healthcare facilities.

  • Patient Financial Advocacy

    Companies that identify, recover, and return funds owed to patients while improving transparency, communication, and trust throughout the healthcare financial experience.

  • Operational Excellence

    Organizations focused on efficient processes, workforce effectiveness, financial discipline, compliance, and sustainable growth that allows providers to concentrate on delivering exceptional care.

  • Community Impact

    Healthcare businesses that strengthen the communities they serve through improved access to care, operational stability, and responsible stewardship of healthcare resources.

  • Long-Term Partnership

    Organizations seeking a strategic partner committed to building enduring value through investment, leadership support, operational expertise, and responsible growth.
